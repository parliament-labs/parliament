# Naming Conventions for Project Parliament

In developing Project Parliament, significant thought was given to the naming conventions for the project and its main components, Tyto and Noctua. These names reflect not only the technical aspects of the project but also the passion and dedication invested in it.

## Project Parliament

The overarching name "Parliament" was inspired by the term for a group of owls. It symbolizes wisdom, insight, and cohesive collaboration, qualities that resonate with the core objectives of the project.

### Symbolism of Owls

Owls are often associated with wisdom and knowledge. By linking the project to these majestic creatures, we emphasize the thoughtful and innovative approach taken to create this modern database management client.

## Project Tyto

"Tyto" refers to a genus of barn owls, known for their distinctive heart-shaped face. The choice of this name is tied to the passion and commitment put into the frontend development of the project. This heart-shaped feature symbolizes the love and dedication invested in creating a user-facing portion that's both elegant and streamlined. Tyto represents the empathy and care that will guide our design and interaction, ensuring an experience that resonates with the users' needs and expectations.

## Project Noctua

"Noctua" is the genus for the little owl, often associated with the Greek goddess Athena. This name was chosen for the backend component, reflecting the idea that much of the development would occur at night as a side project. Noctua symbolizes the diligence and meticulous care put into the engine driving the project's functionality.

## Conclusion

The names Parliament, Tyto, and Noctua were chosen with careful consideration, weaving together the technical, philosophical, and personal aspects of the project. This rich tapestry of meaning adds depth to the project and serves as a constant reminder of the core values and aspirations that guide our development.

We hope that these names inspire all who work on and use Project Parliament, guiding us toward success in our quest to create an innovative and user-friendly database management client.
